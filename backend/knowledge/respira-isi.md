# Respira HFA Important Safety Information

## Important Safety Information
Respira HFA is not for acute bronchospasm. Paradoxical bronchospasm may occur; discontinue immediately if it does. Use with caution in narrow-angle glaucoma and urinary retention. The most common adverse reactions were dry mouth, pharyngitis and sinusitis. See full Prescribing Information.
