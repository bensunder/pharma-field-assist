# Cardivex Access and Coverage FAQ

## Formulary Coverage
Cardivex is covered on Tier 2 for the major national commercial PBMs as of Q4 2026. Prior authorization is generally not required for commercially insured adults with documented primary hyperlipidemia.

## Copay Savings Program
Eligible commercially insured patients may pay as little as $10 per 30-day fill with the Cardivex Savings Card. The program is not valid for patients enrolled in Medicare, Medicaid or any other federal or state healthcare program.

## Samples
Reps may provide Cardivex 10 mg starter samples only to licensed prescribers, with a signed sample receipt captured in CRM within 24 hours.
