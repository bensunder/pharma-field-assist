# Respira HFA (tiolanide) — Prescribing Summary

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY.

## Indications and Usage
Respira HFA is a long-acting muscarinic antagonist indicated for the once-daily maintenance treatment of airflow obstruction in adults 40 years and older with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Respira is not indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm.

## Dosage and Administration
Two inhalations (2.5 mcg each) once daily at the same time each day. Do not exceed two inhalations in 24 hours. Prime the inhaler before first use by releasing three test sprays.

## Clinical Efficacy
In the 24-week AIRWAY-2 study, Respira improved trough FEV1 by 118 mL versus placebo at week 24.
