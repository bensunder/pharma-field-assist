# Cardivex (velostatin) — Prescribing Summary

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY.

## Indications and Usage
Cardivex is an HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor indicated as an adjunct to diet to reduce LDL-C in adults 18 years and older with primary hyperlipidemia or mixed dyslipidemia.

## Dosage and Administration
The recommended starting dose is 10 mg once daily, taken at any time of day with or without food. The dose range is 5 mg to 40 mg once daily. Assess LDL-C 4 to 12 weeks after initiation or titration and adjust accordingly. Patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl below 30 mL/min) should start at 5 mg and not exceed 10 mg daily.

## Dosage Forms and Strengths
Tablets: 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg and 40 mg film-coated tablets.

## Contraindications
Cardivex is contraindicated in patients with acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis, and in patients with hypersensitivity to velostatin or any excipient.

## Clinical Efficacy
In the 12-week VELO-1 trial (n=1,840), Cardivex 10 mg reduced LDL-C by a mean of 41% from baseline versus 3% for placebo. At 40 mg the mean LDL-C reduction was 54%.

## Drug Interactions
Avoid concomitant use with cyclosporine and gemfibrozil. Limit Cardivex to 10 mg daily when used with clarithromycin.
