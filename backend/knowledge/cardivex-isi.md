# Cardivex Important Safety Information

## Important Safety Information
Cardivex is contraindicated in acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis. Myopathy and rhabdomyolysis have been reported; risk increases with higher doses and certain concomitant drugs. Advise patients to promptly report unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness. Hepatic enzyme abnormalities may occur; measure liver enzymes before initiation and as clinically indicated. Increases in HbA1c and fasting glucose have been reported. Most common adverse reactions (2% or more) were nasopharyngitis, myalgia, arthralgia and headache. See full Prescribing Information.
