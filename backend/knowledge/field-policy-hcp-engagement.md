# Field Policy — HCP Engagement

## Meals and Hospitality
Meals provided to HCPs must be modest, occasional, and accompanied by a scientific or educational presentation. The per-person limit is $125 including tax and tip. Meals may not be provided to spouses or guests of the HCP.

## Speaker Programs
Speaker programs must use only MLR-approved slide decks. Reps may not modify slides or present clinical data outside the approved deck.

## Unsolicited Off-Label Requests
If an HCP asks an unsolicited question about an unapproved use, the rep must not answer. Submit a Medical Information Request (MIR) in CRM so Medical Affairs can respond directly.

## Adverse Event Reporting
Any adverse event a rep becomes aware of, from any source, must be reported to Pharmacovigilance within 24 hours, even if the rep is unsure whether it is related to the product.
